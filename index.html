Research

My undergraduate thesis at BUET studied how magnesium changes the magnetic behavior of a manganite.

Effect of Mg doping on La0.6MgxCa1-xMnO3

Three compositions (x = 0, 0.1, 0.2) were made by solid-state reaction. Room temperature XRD showed an orthorhombic structure in every sample. More Mg shrinks the lattice parameters and Mn-O-Mn bond angles, which weakens ferromagnetism through the double-exchange mechanism.

All samples show a second-order ferromagnetic to paramagnetic transition. The magnetocaloric effect, measured through entropy change and relative cooling power, grows with Mg content.

Curie temperature
281.36 K down to 220.5 K
Entropy change and cooling power
Both increase with Mg
Method
Solid-state reaction, XRD, PPMS
